# Naaza Erisuis

## Identidad y afiliación

- **ID estable (legado):** `CHAR-DARK-NAAZA`; the historical-looking prefix is not proof of Era Oscura participation.
- **Nombre japonés reportado:** ナァーザ・エリスイス.
- **Alias reportado:** «Miyal Hound»; its literal translation and Japanese reading should be checked.
- **Especie y función reportadas:** Chienthrope, pharmacist and captain of Miach Familia.
- **Progresión:** the wiki reports both Level 2 and Level 3 data from different points in the published sequence. They belong to different stages; this file does not treat them as simultaneous.

## Appearance

The English wiki describes long brown hair, purple eyes, dog ears and a tail. It presents the silver right arm as a prosthesis. The reported height (159 cm) and age (18) are summary data whose temporal basis must be documented before they become fixed values.

## History and psychological consequences

The community summary says Naaza lost her original right arm during an incident in the Dungeon after monsters severely injured her limbs. It links the experience to a severe fear response around monsters and to her stopping frontline adventuring. This is a useful behavioral lead because it connects a past event with a present limitation, but the exact injuries, scene, timing and symptoms still need a primary locator.

## Work, skills and limitations

- **Occupation:** potion-making / pharmacy and customer service.
- **Reported magic:** *Darbh Daol* (ダルヴ・ダオル), described by the wiki as releasing black particles that weaken a target's Status; using it reportedly causes backlash to Naaza.
- **Reported equipment:** bow; her long-range accuracy and rapid shots are described in the community profile.
- **Development Ability:** Mixing (調合), related to the quality of prepared items.
- **Crafting / healing context:** the silver prosthesis is described in community summaries as having been commissioned through the Dian Cecht Familia and made by Airmid. The wording is not yet tied to a primary passage, so keep maker, requester, lender and institution as separate roles until checked.

## Personality and motivations

The wiki presents Naaza as intelligent and capable but financially unscrupulous in earlier dealings with Bell, including diluting potions or pressuring him to buy. It attributes this conduct partly to her desire to repair the financial situation of Miach Familia after its other members reportedly left because of the prosthesis debt. The same summaries describe later gratitude toward Bell, protective feelings toward Miach and jealousy. This is a secondary synthesis, not a line-by-line assessment of the novel.

## Relationships and continuity

- **Miach:** central attachment, both familial and reported romantic.
- **Bell:** customer whom she initially manipulates and later helps.
- **Dian Cecht / Airmid:** linked to the prosthesis and to professional rivalry; keep business arrangement, personal resentment and romance rivalries distinct.
- **Astrea Record:** no located scene confirms she participates in the central historical interval.
