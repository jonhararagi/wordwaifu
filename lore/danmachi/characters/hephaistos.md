# Hephaistos

## Identidad y afiliación

- **ID estable (legado):** `CHAR-DARK-HEPHAISTOS`; retained for compatibility, not as confirmation of Era Oscura participation.
- **Nombre japonés:** ヘファイストス. The Spanish wiki uses *Hephaistos*, while the English page title uses *Hephaestus*; the project keeps one ID and records both variants.
- **Species / role:** goddess and master smith; head of Hephaistos Familia.
- **Alias reported:** «Divine Smith» / «Herrera Divina».

## Appearance

The community summaries describe red hair, a red eye and an eyepatch covering a defect in the right eye. A reported height is 165 cm. The description is secondary and does not attempt to diagnose or reinterpret the fictional deformity.

## Personality and professional standards

The English and Spanish pages characterize Hephaistos as strict, hardworking, composed and serious in professional matters. They report that she protects the reputation of her Familia and refuses to lower the quality of its products simply to satisfy a request. The same summaries describe affection toward Familia members and personal insecurity regarding her appearance. These details are summarized from community pages rather than transcribed from dialogue.

## Craft and notable work

The community wiki credits her with forging the Hestia Knife, described as a living weapon carrying Hestia's Falna, without using her arcanum. The exact scene and wording should be checked in the novel. The Spanish page also mentions divine smithing flames but cites a Memoria Freese event; this detail remains scoped to that game/event until a novel source is identified.

## Relationships

- **Hestia:** professional peer and associated with the Hestia Knife commission.
- **Welf Crozzo:** the wiki reports a personal romantic development and her shy response to him; that scene should be checked in its publication context.
- **Hephaistos Familia:** she is described as caring about its members as well as the quality and reputation of their craft.

## Continuity and outstanding questions

The main-line and Sword Oratoria profile is useful independently of the historical character census. No individual page/scene has been located here to establish a role in the central Astrea Record story. The old path under `characters/dark-era/` reflected an unverified candidate roster, not proof.
