# DANMACHI-RESEARCH-006 · Hestia/Bell chronology and AU divergence gate

**Status:** source_checked for the specific statements below; the complete novel chronology remains partial.  
**Scope:** validate the timing assumptions that affect the author's OC premise. This is a research note, not an assertion that every date on fan wikis is official.

## Findings with evidence

### Claim A — Bell is already Hestia's sole familia member at the opening incident

- **Evidence:** the official anime site's synopsis for episode 1 says Bell has been Hestia's only familia member for half a month when the Minotaur incident occurs.
- **Source:** https://danmachi.com/danmachi/story/episode1.html
- **What this supports:** a relative interval for Bell's membership in Hestia Familia at the opening incident in the anime continuity.
- **What this does not prove:** the exact date Hestia descended to the lower world, or the full chronology of the light novel in every detail.

### Claim B — Astrea Record is presented as taking place seven years before the main story's reference point

- **Evidence:** the official series chronology describes the Astrea Record story as “seven years ago” and names the “Dark Age of Orario” (オラリオの暗黒期).
- **Source:** https://danmachi.com/chronology/
- **What this supports:** the approximate historical anchor for the Astrea Record story as presented on the official portal.
- **What this does not prove:** that “Dark Age” is interchangeable with “Golden Age”, or the exact date of every event within that period.

### Claim C — The six-month Hestia descent interval is secondary-source information in the current research set

- **Evidence:** the community timeline lists Hestia's descent six months before the start of the series and her move to the abandoned church three months before the start.
- **Source:** https://danmachi.fandom.com/wiki/Timeline
- **Authority:** community-maintained secondary source, not sufficient on its own to approve a canonical date.
- **Required follow-up:** locate the corresponding statement in a legally accessible primary source or an official synopsis before marking the interval as cross_checked/approved.

## Consequence for the author's AU premise

The proposed OC arrives five years before the main story, meets Hestia, and may later be recruited by Loki and called “Bell”. This cannot silently be treated as canon-compatible. It needs an explicit AU decision:

1. **Keep the five-year arrival:** mark the early Hestia encounter as an AU override and record its consequences.
2. **Keep the canonical Hestia timeline:** move the encounter to a compatible date or make the early meeting a different character/event.
3. **Clarify the reference point:** state which event “five years before” refers to.
4. **Resolve identity:** explicitly choose whether Bell Cranel still exists separately, the OC replaces/reinterprets Bell, or “Bell” is an AU nickname.

No option is selected automatically. The author retains control of the divergence.
